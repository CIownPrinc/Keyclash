How to play

  • ⌨️

    Type a word on the map next to your land to march on it. No clicking needed.

  • 🛡️

    Dots mean strength. Strong hexes take a few words. Type your own border words to dig in.

  • 🔥

    Keep a chain by typing a word every 3 seconds. At 3 you hit twice as hard, at 6 each capture spills onto open land.

  • 🏰

    Stay connected. Land cut off from your castle scores nothing. Castles can't fall.

  • 💰

    Grab bounties for 2 extra points that stay with the hex. Your commander earns an airdrop 🪂 every 4, 5, or 6 words; it takes open land two steps away.

  • ✦

    Check the round contract. It gives both players the same bonus target, like supplied shrines, connected land, or enemy ground you can hold.

  • ♟

    Your commander shapes your army. Vanguard hits enemy ground harder, Quartermaster earns air drops faster, and Warden builds tougher frontline hexes.
